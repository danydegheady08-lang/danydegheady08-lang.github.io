I study Mechatronics Engineering at SRH Hochschule Berlin and I am in my final semester. Mechatronics combines mechanics, electronics and software, and over the degree I have been drawn most to the software side of production systems: how a line is controlled, how its state is recorded, and how an operator or a manager can see what is going on.

In the Advanced Programming course I built a simulated ball pen production line in Python, with an operator screen, a time series database and a Grafana dashboard. I also practised version control on a Space Invaders game and a recipe collection. My final project is a simulated spark plug line, described on the projects page.
