The spark plug line.

A made-to-order line for business customers who sell plugs under their own brand. Seven stations, one number that decides everything: the electrode gap.

Read the plan
01ShellCuts the thread on the steel blank
02InsulatorPresses and sinters the ceramic body
03Center electrodeCuts and forms the electrode
04Ground electrodeWelds to the shell, sets the gap
05AssemblySeals the insulator into the shell
06Final QCMeasures the gap, tests the plug
07PackagingPacks the lot with the customer's brand and lot number

Tools

Python, Git and GitHub, state machines, Tkinter, InfluxDB, Flux, Grafana, Docker Compose, pytest, OEE and ISA-95 basics.
